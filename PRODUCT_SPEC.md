# Product contract — V1

- Audience: Hebrew-speaking absolute-beginner English learners who already recognize ABC letters.
- UI language: Hebrew, progressively reduced.
- Audio: TTS only in Hebrew and English; zero recorded audio.
- Learning target: decode and understand novel beginner sentences, not memorize fixed sentences.
- Profiles: 4 fully independent profiles.
- Curriculum: 12 worlds, 84 core lessons, adaptive reviews outside the fixed lesson count.
- Core learning dimensions: listening, recognition, reading, usage.
- Representative targets:
  - I see a dog.
  - I am happy.
  - They are happy.
  - This is my mom.
  - I like pizza.
  - The ball is under the table.
- Final assessment must combine already learned words into unseen sentences.
